## Article 2 — Scope of Services

### 2.1 Supply Scope

Subject to this Agreement, FogHashing shall, pursuant to executed Statements of Work, design, manufacture, test, and supply to Buyer:

**(a) Immersion Cooling Equipment (AC45 / FOG A Series)** — two-phase and/or single-phase immersion cooling tanks, ancillary thermal control systems ("TCS"), pumps, heat exchangers, sensors, control electronics, and approved dielectric coolant fill, configured for thermal management of PCIe-form-factor GPU servers and similar high-density payloads typical of AI inference workloads, in each case as further specified in Appendix A and the applicable Statement of Work.

**(b) Hydro / Direct Liquid Cooling Equipment (DC45 / FOG D Series)** — coolant distribution units ("CDU"), liquid-to-liquid or liquid-to-air heat exchangers, manifolds, quick-disconnect couplings, leak-detection sensors, control electronics, and treated-water or water-glycol working fluid, configured for thermal management of SXM-form-factor GPU servers and similar high-density payloads typical of AI training workloads, in each case as further specified in Appendix A and the applicable Statement of Work.

If, within ten (10) calendar days following delivery to Buyer's site (the "**Acceptance Window**"), Buyer fails to deliver to FogHashing a written notice of non-conformance that (i) specifically identifies, by reference to the objective acceptance criteria in Appendix B §B7, each respect in which the Equipment fails to conform, (ii) is signed by a duly authorized representative of Buyer, and (iii) is supported by reasonable contemporaneous evidence (photographs, sensor logs, witness statements), then the Equipment shall be conclusively and irrevocably deemed accepted by Buyer ("**Deemed Acceptance**"), and Buyer shall be deemed to have waived all rights to reject the Equipment, claim non-conformance, or assert any breach of warranty other than warranty claims expressly preserved under Appendix F. The Acceptance Window shall not be extended by reason of any holiday, weekend, personnel availability, Buyer operational schedule, or any other circumstance other than a written extension expressly granted by FogHashing.

### 7.4 Buyer-Caused Site Delay

Without prejudice to FogHashing's other rights and remedies, any delay attributable to Buyer's failure to achieve Site Readiness, to provide vessel nominations, to obtain local permits, to provide trained personnel, to make timely payment, or otherwise to perform its obligations, shall not (a) extend any obligation of FogHashing, (b) extend any warranty period, (c) excuse any payment obligation of Buyer, (d) entitle Buyer to any Service Credit, or (e) constitute a breach by FogHashing.
